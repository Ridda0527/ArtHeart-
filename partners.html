Community Partner

BeMighty Little Rock

Our organization collaborates with BeMighty Little Rock which is a city-wide anti-hunger campaign aimed at connecting kids, teens, and families to free meals, cooking and nutrition education, food security resources, and opportunities to eat, play and learn.

Together we assemble goody bags and co-host events that bring creativity, nutrition education, and wellness to children across our community!

Learn About BeMighty
Community Partner

Arkansas Period Poverty Project

The Arkansas Period Poverty Project is a Little Rock–based community group founded in 2018 that works to end period poverty and promote menstrual equity across Arkansas through donations, education, and legislation — regularly donating period products to schools statewide so students don't have to miss class for lack of access.

We're proud to stand alongside them in supporting the health, dignity, and wellness of young people across our community!

Learn About the Project
Podcast Partner

BeyondTheBlue

BeyondTheBlue is an organization dedicated to mental health and physical wellness. We recently teamed up with them to record a podcast episode all about art — how it sparks creativity and why making art is so good for our minds and hearts.

Give it a listen and hear how creativity can help all of us heal and grow!

Community Partner

Access 2 Smiles

We partnered with Access 2 Smiles to create dental health craft kits that teach kids how to take care of their teeth and keep their smiles healthy — all through fun, hands-on art projects.

Together we're helping kids build healthy habits that keep their smiles bright!
