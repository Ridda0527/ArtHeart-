What is Art from the Heart?

We're a student-led club from Little Rock Central High School dedicated to bringing art, wellness, and joy to kids across our community — completely free!

We assemble creative goody bags and hand them out to visitors at the Central Arkansas Library System. Each kit comes with a fun project that teaches kids about health, the human body, and mental well-being.
