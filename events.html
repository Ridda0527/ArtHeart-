Our Journey

Every event brings us closer to our community

Dental Health Craft Kits with Access 2 Smiles

Photos coming soon

Together with Access 2 Smiles, we created dental health craft kits that teach kids how to take care of their teeth and keep their smiles healthy — all through fun, hands-on art!

9/10/26 Fall Interest Meeting

Kicking off a brand-new school year! At our fall interest meeting, we welcomed new members and shared everything coming up — craft kit assemblies and library booths, nonprofit collabs, podcasts and videos, health awareness pamphlets, donation drives, and a hospital collab. We can't wait to see what this year brings!

4/20/26 Period Product Care Package Assembly

We gathered at the LRCH Library to sort donated period products into care packages for people in our community — because access to basic hygiene supplies is an essential part of staying healthy, confident, and in school.

2/13/26 HOHP x AHI Physical Activity Kits

Together with HOHP, we assembled physical-activity kits that encourage kids to get up, get moving, and have fun — a reminder that staying active is good for both your body and your mood!

1/17/26 Sunglasses Booth at the Library

At our library booth, young visitors decorated their very own sunglasses while learning about eye health — why our eyes need protection from the sun's UV rays and how a good pair of sunglasses helps keep them healthy for years to come.

1/16/26 DIY Sunglasses Kits

We assembled DIY sunglasses kits, complete with gems for decorating! Each kit teaches kids about eye health and UV ray protection — like why you should never look directly at the sun and how sunglasses shield your eyes from harmful rays.

11/15/25 Jellyfish Booth at the Library

At the library, kids made our “Relax Like a Jellyfish” craft — writing positive affirmations on the jellyfish's tentacles — and strung their own positive affirmation bracelets: little reminders to breathe, slow down, and be kind to themselves.

10/17/25 Jellyfish Mental Health Goody Bags

Our mental health goody bags featured positive affirmation bracelets and a “Relax Like a Jellyfish” craft with uplifting affirmations written on each tentacle — helping kids build calming habits and a positive mindset.

4/9/25 Mini Garden-Making Kits

Members put together “My Little Garden” kits so kids could grow their own plants at home — a hands-on way to learn about nature, patience, and caring for something living.

3/18/25 Maraca Goody Bags

We assembled maraca kits to celebrate the power of music therapy! Inside the hospital, music helps patients cope with stress, pain, and long stays; outside the hospital, it's a joyful, healthy way for anyone to express their feelings, relax, and connect with others.

3/4/25 Introductory Meeting

Where it all began! We introduced Art from the Heart to our first members, shared our mission of bringing art and wellness to kids in our community, and celebrated with snacks and goody bags.
